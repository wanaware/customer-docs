## Transcript

A relationship view answers a practical question: what is connected to this asset, and how? Start in Assets, then Inventory.

Search for the fictional asset `demo-web-01` and open it. Check the asset name and type before drawing conclusions from its connections.

Open Relationship or Graph. The focused asset is the starting node. Connected nodes represent related assets, and lines represent the relationships currently known to WanAware.

Select a connected asset to inspect its label and details. Use available filters or depth controls to reduce noise, but remember that a filter can also hide an expected connection.

If a relationship seems missing, clear filters and confirm both assets exist before reporting a data problem. Do not delete an asset to force the graph to refresh.

Use the written guide for exact checks and the Support evidence list. Include asset IDs and a screenshot, but never include source-system credentials.
