## Transcript

Welcome to WanAware. In the next two minutes, we will find one asset, check what it is, and follow one useful relationship.

Launchpad is the starting view. Assets contains the detailed records we will use for this path. Administration appears only when your role allows it.

Open Assets, then Inventory. Search for the documentation asset `Docs Demo Web 01` and clear any existing filters.

Open the asset and confirm its name, Asset Type, source context, Data Tags, and Structure Tags. Tabs depend on the Asset Type and your role.

Open Relationship Graph. The focused node is the asset you selected. Follow one line to a connected resource and select it to confirm its identity.

If an expected connection is absent, clear filters before reporting a missing relationship. Changing the view does not change the underlying data.

You have completed the path when you can name the asset, its type, and one connected resource. The written guide has the current steps and support checklist.
