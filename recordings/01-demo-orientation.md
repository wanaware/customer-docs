**Target length:** 2 minutes 15 seconds

- Sign in as a normal customer user.
- Remove bookmarks, notifications, email addresses, and account identifiers from view.
- Confirm the navigation and labels match the released product.
- Record at 1080p and keep the pointer visible.

## Transcript

Welcome to WanAware. In the next two minutes, you will find an asset, open its details, and see how it relates to the rest of your environment.

The Launchpad is your starting point. Functions contains the work areas you will use most: Structures, Assets, Observability, Elements, and Workers. Administration is visible only when your role allows it.

Open Assets and select Inventory. Inventory shows assets that have been accepted into your managed environment. Discovery may contain assets that still need review.

Search for the fictional asset named `demo-web-01`. You can also filter the list when you know an asset type, status, or another property. Clear filters if an expected result is missing.

Select the asset. The detail page brings its identity, properties, and available actions together. The actions you see depend on your role and enabled product features.

Open the Relationship or Graph view. The selected asset is the focus. Connected nodes show related assets, and lines show the relationships WanAware can currently establish from available data.

You have reached the first useful outcome: finding an asset and understanding its context. The written guide beside this video contains the authoritative steps and troubleshooting checks.

If your screen differs, use the article’s support checklist. Never send passwords, credentials, access tokens, or private keys.
