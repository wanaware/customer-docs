# Monitor and investigate

**Observability** shows the monitoring coverage and results for assets, sites, Elements, and Collections. **Workers** run monitoring work. **Pulse** adds diagnostic context when your account and role include Pulse access.

Configure a single known target first. Confirm that the monitor is active and producing results before expanding coverage.
