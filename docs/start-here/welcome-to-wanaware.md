WanAware brings your Assets, their business context, and their Relationships into one place. Choose the path that matches what you need to accomplish now.

<Cards columns={2}>
  <Card title="Explore WanAware" href="https://docs.wanaware.com/docs/your-first-30-minutes" icon="fa-magnifying-glass">
    **Outcome:** Find a known Asset, understand its details, and follow one useful Relationship.

    - **For:** Anyone exploring an existing workspace
    - **Permission:** `read my_launchpad` and `read assets`
    - **Time:** 10–30 minutes
  </Card>
  <Card title="Build your environment" href="https://docs.wanaware.com/docs/administrator-setup-path" icon="fa-diagram-project">
    **Outcome:** Add data, define how Assets are described, and organize them for your team.

    - **For:** Customer administrators
    - **Permission:** Administrative permissions vary by task
    - **Time:** Start with a 15-minute setup sequence
  </Card>
</Cards>

![Relationship Graph showing an example Asset, grouping controls, connected nodes, and hop-depth choices.](https://files.readme.io/e7db6b94b13b42103b48e405a82aa443fbbba2af036bdbfc2996c4d8b8170c41-asset-relationship-graph.png)

The quickest way to understand the workspace is to open a known Asset and follow one connection in its **Relationship Graph**. If the workspace has no data yet, start with the administrator path.

## Access varies by role

WanAware uses Role-based access. People in the same company may see different pages and actions.

If an instruction names a missing action, ask a customer administrator to check your Role. Use [Missing pages, actions, or permissions](https://docs.wanaware.com/docs/missing-pages-actions-or-permissions) before treating the issue as a product failure.

## Something is not working

Start with [Troubleshooting and support](https://docs.wanaware.com/docs/troubleshooting-and-support). Choose the article that matches the visible symptom or exact error before changing configuration or deleting data.

## Get help

Email [support@wanaware.com](mailto:support@wanaware.com?subject=WanAware%20documentation%20question) with your company, affected user, page URL, timestamp and time zone, and the outcome you were trying to reach. Never send passwords, credentials, access tokens, secrets, or private keys.
